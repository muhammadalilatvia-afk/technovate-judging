How judging works

1 · Team on stage

The organizer selects the team that is presenting. Every judge's screen switches to it automatically.

2 · Judges score

5 criteria, 0–10 each: innovation, real-world value, feasibility, market potential and presentation.

3 · Live ranking

The team's score is the judges' average out of 100. Ties are broken by the innovation score.
